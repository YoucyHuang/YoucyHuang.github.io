Paintings

Semi-transparent · Authenticity · Landscape

My painting practice investigates materiality and presence. The semi-transparent mountain series layers acrylic, silk, and light to make visible what lies behind the visible — a meditation on distance, memory, and the irreversibility of gesture. The authenticity series works from direct observation, capturing subjects in moments outside performative self-presentation.
