Writing

Essays · Fiction · Poetry

A. Critical Essays

  • The Scholar-Official’s Utopia (士大夫的乌托邦) 2024 Subjectivity and symbolic violence in Chinese literati and European aristocratic portraiture — a comparative analysis of who gets to display and who is displayed.
  • The Emperor of Quotations: Cynicism’s Golden Shield (名言皇帝) 2025 How famous quotes function as authority surrogates in Chinese and Western public discourse — when “who said it” replaces “what was said.”
  • From Field to Self-Cultivation: New Confucian Ethics’ Spatial Generative Path (从场域到修身) 2025 Proposing an inverted path — morality guiding self-cultivation through designed spatial environments, with AI as a dynamic facilitator of ethical atmospheres.
  • Water-based Materials, Temporal Structure, and Ganxing (水性材料与感兴) 2025 How the irreversible material logic of ink painting trains a subject who bears consequences in flowing time — ganxing as a generative method, not just a historical concept.
  • From Robert Ryman: Body, Trace, and Presence in Painting 2025 MFA thesis. Ryman’s white paintings as an inquiry into materiality, the body’s irreducible presence, and the event of viewing — a painter’s phenomenology of the surface.
  • Involution and Lying Flat: Youth Subjectivity in Contemporary China (内卷与躺平) 2025 Structural analysis of how compressed modernization severed traditional meaning structures without building new ones — and what young people do when “effort→reward” breaks.
  • The Aphasia of Contemporary Art (失语的当代艺术) 2025 Guaren Project core essay. Diagnosing art’s loss of a position of responsibility under neoliberal governance — and proposing channel-type art practice as an alternative.
  • Witness and Transformation: Sebastião Salgado’s Documentary Photography 2024 Academic paper on the ethics of aestheticizing suffering — can beauty serve truth in documentary practice, or does it inevitably filter and consume what it claims to witness?
  • Seeing Mountains as Mountains Again: Zao Wou-Ki’s Three Metamorphoses 2024 Tracing Zao Wou-Ki’s journey through three stages of East-West synthesis — from symbolic insertion to spontaneous expression to beyond the question of East and West.

B. Fiction

  • Family Banquet (家宴) 2025–2026  ·  Novel The dining table as courtroom — mother-daughter love disguised as criticism, silence as the only speech that carries no guilt. The blood in the refrigerator seeped in over decades.
  • The Last Letter to Mother (给母亲的最后一封信) 2024  ·  Novella, 16,095 words A daughter’s unsent letter. When “I did everything for you” becomes a moral position that ends all argument, what remains is a letter addressed to a name, not a title.
  • Qingming, Received (清茗，收) 2025  ·  AI-Assisted Short Film Script Meta-narrative: an AIGC creator tries to adapt her novel with AI. The AI can generate perfect images but cannot understand standing below a window without going up. So she speaks the parts AI cannot.
  • Blue Stops (蓝止) 2025  ·  Guizhou Batik · Short Film Script A batik designer returns to her Miao village after years away. Tradition, innovation, and a letter that uses her teacher’s name instead of her title. The reply is one word: “好.”

C. Poetry

  • Selected Poems: I and Them · Confession of a Clear Wind · Silent Night 2023–2024 Early poems — the sea, bodies, death, silence, and the distance between people standing on the same shore.
  • Selected Poems: Rebirth · The Small Dark Room · Growth · Returning Home 2025–2026 Later poems — mother, cracks, the well that thirsts, the sun splitting the earth in two. More bone. Less fog.
