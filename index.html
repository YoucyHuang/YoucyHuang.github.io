黄鈺茜 — Artist, Writer & Researcher

I work across painting, photography, writing, and digital media. My practice is grounded in a sustained inquiry into materiality, temporality, and the conditions under which meaning emerges — or fails to emerge — in contemporary life. Trained in pure art (BFA & MFA, China Academy of Art), I am currently expanding my practice into digital humanities and computational methods, seeking new forms for old questions.

My theoretical work develops ganxing (感兴) — a concept rooted in Chinese ink painting's irreversible material logic — as both a historical analytic and a generative method for contemporary practice. My fiction and poetry examine family, memory, and the weight of unspoken inheritances. My public art projects explore what it means for art to hold a “position of responsibility” in communities it serves.
