黄鈺茜 — Artist, Writer & Researcher

I am a research-based painter completing my MFA at the China Academy of Art after fifteen years of practice. I work across painting, fiction, poetry, and art theory. My practice and my thinking developed together — from early realism through modern and postmodern philosophy to an ongoing inquiry into materiality, embodiment, and the conditions under which meaning emerges in contemporary life.

I am currently preparing to enter the Digital Humanities — not to leave art behind, but because I have arrived at a question that art alone cannot answer: how the trained body of a painter can contribute to building digital cultural heritage that is not only informative, but alive.
